FO World View is an unofficial, independent, fan-made mapping and tabletop
gaming utility. It is not affiliated with, sponsored, approved, or endorsed by
Bethesda Softworks, ZeniMax Media, Microsoft, or Modiphius Entertainment.
Fallout and related names, marks, settings, and other franchise material belong
to their respective owners. FO World View is not an official or
franchise-licensed Fallout product.

The public release contains original software, original interface artwork,
project-created map schematics, structured reference facts, source locators,
identifiers, coordinates, and original short summaries. It does not include
proprietary game files, copied game-map pixels, extracted source prose, private
research ledgers, community KML/KMZ packages, or private map imports.

The optional Local Map Builder operates only on a user's own supported local
game installation. Its locally generated outputs remain on that user's device
and are not distributed by this repository.

FO World View is distributed free of charge. Optional support funds original
software development, accessibility, testing, research tooling, and release
infrastructure. A donation does not purchase or unlock proprietary game assets,
extracted maps, source text, or franchise content.
